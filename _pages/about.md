After completion of my BSc in Microbiology & Immunology at Dalhousie University, I continued in the department as an MSc student supervised by Dr. Denys Khaperskyy. I defended in July 2026, and am currently a first year PhD Candidate at the University of Toronto under the supervision of Dr. Christina Guzzo.

Research Interests: Influenza Pandemic Preparedness, Host-pathogen Interactions, Influenza Host Shutoff, Antiviral Resistance.
